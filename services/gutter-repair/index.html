Common Gutter Problems We Fix

We repair all types of gutter issues including leaks at seams or joints, sagging or pulling away from the fascia, loose or missing hangers, damaged or dented sections, clogged or damaged downspouts, improper slope causing standing water, gutter separation at connections, and rust or corrosion on metal gutters. We also repair fascia boards that have rotted due to gutter overflow, and we can reattach gutters that have pulled away from the house. Each repair is performed with quality materials for lasting results.

Our Gutter Repair Process

We start with a thorough inspection of your entire gutter system to identify all issues. We check for proper slope, secure attachment, and clear downspouts. We then provide a detailed repair plan with clear pricing. Repairs may include resealing leaks with professional-grade sealant, replacing damaged sections with new seamless material, installing new hangers at proper intervals, adjusting slope for proper drainage, and clearing or replacing downspouts. We test the system after repairs to ensure everything is working properly.

When to Repair vs. Replace

Not all gutter problems require full replacement. Minor leaks, loose hangers, and isolated damage can usually be repaired. However, if your gutters are more than 20 years old, have extensive damage, or are the old sectional type with multiple leaking seams, replacement may be more cost-effective. Signs Your Gutters Need Repair

How do you know when your gutters need repair? Look for these signs: water overflowing during rain (indicates clogs or improper slope), sagging or pulling away from the fascia (indicates loose or missing hangers), visible leaks or drips (indicates holes or separated seams), rust or corrosion on metal gutters, peeling paint or water stains on fascia boards, standing water in gutters after rain (indicates improper slope), gutters that are separating at the joints, downspouts that are damaged or disconnected, and water pooling around your foundation. If you notice any of these signs, call us for an inspection. Early repair can prevent more extensive damage and save you money.

Fascia Board Repair and Replacement

When gutters fail, the fascia boards behind them are often the first casualty. Fascia boards are the trim boards that run along the edge of your roof, and they're essential for supporting your gutters. When gutters overflow, water soaks the fascia, causing rot and deterioration. Rotted fascia can't properly support gutters, leading to sagging and detachment. We offer fascia board repair and replacement services. We remove the damaged fascia, install new pressure-treated or primed wood, and properly seal it. We then reattach or install new gutters to the sound fascia. This comprehensive repair ensures your gutter system has a solid foundation for years to come.

When to Replace vs. Repair Your Gutters

Not all gutter problems require full replacement. Minor issues like isolated leaks, loose hangers, or small dents can usually be repaired. However, if your gutters are more than 20 years old, have extensive rust or corrosion, are the old sectional type with multiple leaking seams, or have been damaged by falling trees or ice dams, replacement may be more cost-effective. Frequently Asked Questions About Gutter Repair

How much does gutter repair cost?

Repair costs vary depending on the issue. Minor leak repairs start around $100-200, while more extensive repairs like section replacement range from $200-500. We provide free estimates.

Can sagging gutters be fixed?

Yes, sagging gutters can usually be fixed by installing new hangers at proper intervals and adjusting the slope. In some cases, the fascia board behind the gutter may need repair first.

How do you fix a leaking gutter seam?

For seamless gutters, leaks are rare but can be sealed with professional-grade gutter sealant. For sectional gutters with multiple leaking seams, replacement with seamless gutters is often the better solution.
