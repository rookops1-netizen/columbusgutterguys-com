Downspout Repair in Columbus, OH

Downspouts are the unsung heroes of your gutter system — they carry water from your gutters down to the ground and away from your foundation. When downspouts fail, water can pool around your foundation, causing basement flooding, foundation damage, and erosion. At Columbus Gutter Guys, we provide professional downspout repair and replacement services throughout Columbus and Central Ohio. We fix damaged, clogged, or improperly positioned downspouts to ensure your gutter system works as it should.

Common Downspout Problems

We repair all types of downspout issues including damaged or dented downspout sections, separated connections at elbows or joints, clogged downspouts causing water backup, downspouts that don't extend far enough from the foundation, missing or damaged downspout extensions, improper downspout placement causing water pooling, and downspouts that have pulled away from the gutter. We also install downspout extensions, splash blocks, and underground drainage systems to carry water safely away from your home.

Our Downspout Repair Process

We start by inspecting your downspouts and the areas where they discharge water. We check for clogs, damage, and proper positioning. We clear any clogs using specialized tools. We repair or replace damaged sections with new material that matches your existing system. We ensure proper connections at all joints. We adjust downspout positioning to direct water away from your foundation. We can install extensions, splash blocks, or underground drainage as needed. We test the system with water to ensure proper flow and drainage.

Downspout Extensions and Drainage

Proper downspout drainage is critical for protecting your foundation. We offer several solutions for directing water away from your home. Downspout extensions are simple, cost-effective solutions that carry water 5-10 feet from the foundation. Splash blocks disperse water over a wider area. Underground drainage systems carry water to a safe discharge point through buried pipes. We recommend the best solution based on your home's layout and soil conditions.

Downspout Placement and Drainage

Proper downspout placement is critical for protecting your foundation. Downspouts should be positioned to carry water at least 5-10 feet away from your foundation. They should discharge onto a surface that allows water to drain away — not onto a paved surface that directs water back toward the house. Downspouts should not discharge onto sidewalks or driveways where water can freeze and create slip hazards in winter. In areas with poor drainage, underground downspout extensions that carry water to a safe discharge point through buried pipes are an excellent solution. We assess your property's drainage patterns and recommend the best downspout placement and drainage solutions for your specific situation.

Underground Downspout Drainage Systems

Underground downspout drainage systems are an elegant solution for directing water away from your foundation without unsightly surface extensions. These systems use buried PVC pipes to carry water from your downspouts to a safe discharge point — typically a dry well, drainage ditch, or storm sewer connection. The pipes are buried below frost depth to prevent freezing. They're virtually invisible once installed. They can carry water much further than surface extensions — 50 feet or more. They eliminate tripping hazards from surface extensions. And they provide superior protection for your foundation by ensuring water is carried well away from the house. We design and install underground drainage systems that meet local codes and provide reliable, long-lasting performance.

Frequently Asked Questions About Downspout Repair

How much does downspout repair cost?

Downspout repairs typically range from $75-300 depending on the extent of damage. Simple repairs like reconnecting a separated joint start around $75, while full downspout replacement ranges from $150-300.

How far should downspouts extend from the foundation?

Downspouts should extend at least 5-10 feet from your foundation to prevent water from pooling near the base of your home. We can install extensions or underground drainage to achieve proper distance.

Can you install underground downspout drainage?

Yes, we install underground drainage systems that carry water from your downspouts to a safe discharge point through buried pipes. This is an excellent solution for homes without adequate space for surface extensions.
