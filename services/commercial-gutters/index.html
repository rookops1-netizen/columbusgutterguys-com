Commercial Gutter Materials

Commercial gutters are available in several materials, each with specific advantages. Aluminum is the most common choice — it's lightweight, rust-proof, and available in a wide range of colors. Steel gutters are stronger and more durable, making them ideal for industrial applications where impact resistance is important. Copper gutters offer a premium appearance and exceptional longevity — they can last 50-100 years. Galvanized steel offers good durability at a moderate price point. Each material has different cost, durability, and appearance characteristics. We recommend the best material based on your building's architecture, budget, and performance requirements.

Commercial Gutter Sizing and Design

Commercial gutter systems must be properly sized to handle the water volume from large roof areas. We calculate the required gutter size based on roof square footage, roof pitch, and local rainfall intensity. Commercial buildings often require 6-inch or larger gutters, and some may need custom-fabricated box gutters. We also design the downspout system to handle the water volume — commercial buildings typically need more downspouts and larger downspouts than residential buildings. Proper sizing ensures your gutter system can handle even the heaviest rainstorms without overflowing. We use industry-standard sizing calculations to design systems that perform reliably.
